# Sand A Sketch

The **Sand A Sketch** is a group project created for the university course "Prototyping Interactive Objects" at Hochschule RheinMain.

It is an interactive drawing device inspired by the classic Etch a Sketch toy. Instead of using a stylus or display, a small magnetic steel ball is moved underneath a thin Plexiglas plate filled with fine sand. As the magnet moves, it drags the steel ball through the sand, creating temporary drawings.

The movement is generated by a custom-built mostly 3d-printed CoreXY mechanism powered by two stepper motors and controlled by an Arduino. A joystick allows intuitive two-dimensional control while a potentiometer adjusts the drawing speed.

CoreXY is a belt-driven positioning mechanism commonly used in high-speed 3D printers.

### Step 1: 3D Printing

[Here](#fabrication-files) you will find all .stl-files for the 3d printed parts. 

1. Download all .STL files.
2. Print all components using PLA filament. (Supports are only required for CoreXY corners)

- Recommended infill: 15%.

### Step 2: Assembling the CoreXY System

***NOTE: There is an assembly video under '\Sand-A-Sketch\Footage\Assembly Videos'***

1. Insert the strong magnet into the 3d-printed magnet carriage.
2. Mount the magnet carriage onto the longer metal rods using the designated holes.
3. Insert the remaining two steel rods into their respective holder pieces using the through holes.
4. Now connect the magnet carriage rods to the holders from the previous step using the non-through hole.
5. Insert the assembled carriage into the designated slots of the 3D-printed frame. Mind the orientation, the longer beams should be used for the sides with the metal rods inside the frame.
6. Mount the four corner pulley assemblies onto the frame.
7. Install both stepper motors into their designated mounts on two of the corner pieces.
8. Route both timing belts according to the CoreXY belt layout. Make sure the belts are not twisted.
9. Attach both belts to the magnetic carriage.
10. Tension the belts until they are tight while ensuring the carriage still moves smoothly.
11. Verify that the carriage can move freely across the entire drawing area without binding.

### Step 3: Assembling the enclosure
1. Glue the corner holders into their respective indentations on the base. They have different designs and are made to only fit into their respective notches. Make sure it fits before gluing.
2. Allow the glue to cure before continuing. Now you can place the CoreXY system onto the corner pieces. Once again mind the orientation. The motor corners have special indentations for a tight fit.
   Do not glue this part! It fits tight enough for it to not move when you do not want it to, but you can still swap out parts of the CoreXY easily or take it out to reach the electronics below.

### Step 4: Assembling the controls
1. Install the holder clip into the lower part of the 3d-printed controller.
2. Place the joystick and the potentiometer inside the indentation in the same part of the controller.
3. Route the cables through the cable channels and feed them through the opening in the holder clip for good cable management.
4. Install the upper part of the 3d-print onto the controller and apply pressure on the corners for a snug fit.
5. Now hang the holder clip onto a beam of the CoreXY system.

### Step 5: Motors, Software & Electronics

1. Install the Arduino and the breadboard inside the base of the enclosure.
2. Connect both stepper motors to their respective motor drivers.
3. The cables of the joystick and potentiometer should dangle just behind the holder clip. Connect them to the breadboard according to the wiring diagram.
4. Connect the power supply to the Arduino.
5. Upload the Arduino sketch to the Arduino.
6. Verify that the magnetic carriage moves smoothly and responds correctly to joystick input.

### Step 6: Assembling the Sand-top
1. Glue the cardboard into the lower holding platform of the 3d-printed sand-top.
2. Fill the tray with fine sand until it reaches approximately half the diameter of the steel ball (e.g., 2 mm for a 4 mm ball).
3. Mount your sand-top onto the corner pieces. Be mindful of using the right orientation.
4. Place the steel ball on top of the sand directly above the magnetic carriage.
5. Place the Plexiglass sheet on the higher holding platform of your sand-top. Be once again mindful of using the right orientation.

### Step 7: Have fun!

1. Draw your first temporary sand image!
2. Shake the **Sand A Sketch** carefully to erase image.
3. Repeat!
