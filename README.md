# Sand A Sketch

![Sand A Sketch - Hero Shot](https://github.com/LamiiaSuu/Sand-A-Sketch/blob/7b3ca9e36b72d740b66ece84b7ee0ffa8d913c48/Footage/Showcase%20Images/Hero%20Shot.png)

The **Sand A Sketch** is a group project created for the university course Prototyping Interactive Objects (Summer 2026) at Hochschule RheinMain – University of Applied Sciences and Arts, Wiesbaden.

It is an **interactive drawing device** inspired by both the classic **Etch A Sketch** toy and **Kinetic Sand tables**. Instead of using a stylus or display, you can use a joystick to guide a small magnetic ball is moved on a plate filled with fine sand. As the magnet moves, it drags the ball through the sand, creating temporary drawings.

The movement is generated by a custom-built, mostly 3d-printed, [CoreXY mechanism](https://corexy.com/). It is powered by two stepper motors controlled by an Arduino. The joystick allows intuitive two-dimensional control while a potentiometer adjusts the drawing speed.

If you want to build your own Sand A Sketch you can! Just follow the instructions below.

## STEP BY STEP INSTRUCTIONS

### Step 1: 3D Printing

1. Download all STL files from the [Fabriction Files](#fabrication-files) section 
2. Print all parts in the indicated quantities

**Recommended printer settings:**
- material: PLA
- layer height: 0.2 mm
- infill: 
	- 100% for [SandASketch_No_Motor_Corner_Bar.stl](stl/CoreXY/Individual/SandASketch_No_Motor_Corner_Bar.stl) 
	- 15% for all other parts

**Supports:** 
- **Required:** 
	- SandASketch_Spool.stl
	- SandASketch_Spacer.stl
- **All other parts** were printed successfully without supports on our printer. Depending on your printer and print settings, you may still want to enable supports for overhangs if necessary.

#### 2.4 Inserting the Motors

You will need:
- 1x assembled CoreXY
- 2x 28BYJ-48 – 5V Stepper Motors

1. Insert a stepper motor into each **Motor Corner**.
2. Press each motor firmly into place until it is fully seated.

**Note**
The fit is intentionally tight to keep the motors in place.

#### 2.6 Installing the Timing Belt

![Installed Timing Belt](https://github.com/LamiiaSuu/Sand-A-Sketch/blob/9d2bca636a51deb7e6d3171ac5e32821100ed17a/Footage/CoreXY/Assembly/CoreXY%20Belts%20installation%20Top-Down.jpg)
Installed Timing Belt

You will need:
- 1x assembled CoreXY with motors & pulleys
- 1x [6mm timing belt & pieces](https://amzn.eu/d/0blO36EH)
- scissors
- superglue

Refernce:  
[Manual CoreXY Mechanism by DrRonny on Thingiverse](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4167459)  

1. Place the CoreXY in front of you with the Motor Corners closest to you.

**Belt 1:**

2. Take the end of the **Timing Belt** and carefully glue it towards the top of the inside closest to you of the **Magnet Holder Bottom**. The teeth of the belt should be showing away from you/the motors.
3. Route the belt around the pulleys exactly as shown in the refernce. 
4. Cut the belt with 2-3 cm of ease.

**Belt 2:**

5. Take the end **Timing Belt** and carefully glue it towards the bottom of the inside furthest away from you of the **Magnet Holder Bottom**. The teeth of the belt should be in the same direction as **Belt 1**.
6. Route the belt around the pulleys exactly as shown in the refernce. 
7. Cut the belt with 2-3 cm of ease.

**Tensioning:**

8. Pull one belts taut, but not too tight. The Side Slider should **not** be pulled towards the inside of the CoreXY.
9. Use superglue to glue it right beside its other end.
10. Repeat for the other belt .
11. Wait for the glue to fully dry.

#### 2.7 Checking tension

- 1x assembled CoreXY with motors, pulleys & timing belt
- fine mechanics oil
- scissors

1. Aplly some fine mechanics oil to all metal rods
2. Test if the **Side Sliders** move smoothly and the two **metal rods** holding the  **Magnet Holder Bottom** are straight. 
3. Test if the **Magnet Holder Bottom** slides smoothly.
4. Cut the excess Timing Belt off.

**Important**
If the metal rods are not straight the CoreXY will **not** work correctly.
In this case you will likely need to **adjust the tension of the Timing Belt** again.

#### 2.8 Adding the magnet

You will need:
- 1x assembled CoreXY
- 1x [SandASketch_Magnet_Holder_Top.stl](stl/CoreXY/Individual/SandASketch_Magnet_Holder_Top.stl) 
- 1x Cube Neodymium Magnet - 8 x 8 x 8 mm 

1. Place the **magnet** into the **Magnet Holder Top**.
2. Press the **Magnet Holder Top** onto the **Magnet Holder Bottom** until it fits snug.

You have finished assembling the CoreXY!

### Step 3: Assembling the Base Enclosure

![Base Enclosure with Corner Holders](https://github.com/LamiiaSuu/Sand-A-Sketch/blob/6cb06aaf5f8eaca17e64170e79de1ecc5a618b9c/Footage/Enclosure%20&%20SandTop/Enclosure%20Base%20Plate%20with%20Corner%20Holders%20installed%20Isometric.jpg)
Base Enclosure with Corner Holders

You will need:
- 1x assembled CoreXY
- 1x [Base Plate.stl](stl/Base%20Enclosure%20%26%20SandTop/Base%20Plate.stl) 
- 2x [Enclosure Corner Holder (Motor).stl](stl/Base%20Enclosure%20%26%20SandTop/Enclosure%20Corner%20Holder%20(Motor).stl) 
- 2x [Enclosure Corner Holder (No Motor).stl](stl/Base%20Enclosure%20%26%20SandTop/Enclosure%20Corner%20Holder%20(No%20Motor).stl)

1. Take the **Motor Corner Holders** and click them onto the fitting corners of the **Base Plate** as shown.
2. Apply some superglue to the other two corners of the **Base Plate**
3. Place the **No-Motor Corner Holders** ontop of the glue and press down. Wipe any excess glue.
4. Before drying, put the CoreXY into the holders. This makes sure that all Corner Holder sit straight.

**Important**
Make sure not to glue the CoreXY into place, this way it can still be taken out later.

### Step 4: Assembling the Controls

You will need:
- 1x assembled CoreXY with enclosure
- 1x [Controller Holder Clip.stl](stl/Controls/Controller%20Holder%20Clip.stl) 
- 1x [Upper Controller Part.stl](stl/Controls/Upper%20Controller%20Part.stl) 
- 1x [Lower Controller Part (Base).stl](stl/Controls/Lower%20Controller%20Part%20(Base).stl) 
- 1x Joystick
- 1x Potentiometer

1. Install the **Holder Clip** into the **Lower Controller Part**.
2. Put **jumper cables** onto the **Joystick** and **Potentiometer**.
3. Place the **Potentiometer** into the left indentation and the **Joystick** into the right indentation of the **Lower Controller Part** with the jumper cables going towards the Holder Clip. Like this:
![Placement in Controller](https://github.com/LamiiaSuu/Sand-A-Sketch/blob/950725174d556ea8362970a9bd2da8ccc3edf0fa/Footage/Controller/Disassembled%20Controller.jpg)

4. Pull the free end of the **jumper cables** through the hole of the **Holder Clip**.
5. Press the **Upper Controller Part** onto the **Lower Controller Part** until it clicks into place.
6. Hang the **Holder Clip** onto the **Side Bar** of the CoreXY between both motors.


### Step 5: Assembling the Sand-Top

![Sand-Top with cardboard](https://github.com/LamiiaSuu/Sand-A-Sketch/blob/6cb06aaf5f8eaca17e64170e79de1ecc5a618b9c/Footage/Enclosure%20&%20SandTop/Carton%20glued%20into%20SandTop.jpg)
Sand-Top with cardboard

You will need:
- 1x [SandTop.stl](stl/Base%20Enclosure%20%26%20SandTop/SandTop.stl) 
- Thick cardboard
- Fine sand/Quartz sand
- cutter knife
- cutting mat
- super glue

1. Cut the **cardboard** to fit onto the lower inner edge of the **Sand-Top**.
2. Glue the **cardboard** onto the lower inner edge of the **Sand-Top** with **superglue**.
3. Wait until the glue has dried.
4. Fill the Sand-Top with **sand** until it reaches approximately half the diameter of the magnetic ball (roughly 4 mm).

Put this to the side for now.

### Step 6: Wiring

![Wiring Diagram](https://github.com/LamiiaSuu/Sand-A-Sketch/blob/d723adf54325dd6d9a1a2566c7abda9210eca9b4/Footage/Circuit.png)
Wiring Diagram

You will need:
- 1x assembled CoreXY with enclosure & controls
- 1x Arduino Nano
- 1x Arduino Nano Power Cable
- 1x Breadboard
- Jumper Cables
- *(Optional)* 5× [Cable Channel.stl](stl/Base%20Enclosure%20%26%20SandTop/Cable%20Channel.stl)

1. Place your **Arduino Nano** on your **breadboard** and connect the **power cable**.
2. Place your **breadboard** into the **base** of the enclosure.
3. Use the **wiring diagramm** above to connect the **motors** and **controls** to the **Arduino Nano** on the breadboard.

*Optional*
You can use some **Cable Channels** to secure your **jumper cables**:

4. Arrange your **jumper cables**.
5. Place some **superglue** on the bottom of a **Cable Channel**.
6. Press the **Cable Channel** wherever you want to place it.

**Important**
Always double **check** your components **datasheets** when wiring. Accidentally swapping ground and volt can damage your components.

### Step 7: Code

You will need:
- a computer
- the [Arduino IDE](https://docs.arduino.cc/software/ide/)
- the [CheapStepper Library (v.0.2.1) by Tyler Henry](https://docs.arduino.cc/libraries/cheapstepper/)
- the [Code](#code) 

1. Install the **Arduino IDE**.
2. Install the **CheapStepper Library** (v.0.2.1) by Tyler Henry in the Arduino IDE.
3. Download the **code** and open it in the Arduino IDE
4. Connect the **Arduino Nano** to your computer
5. Select **Arduino Nano** as the board and the correct **port**
6. Upload the **code** onto the Arduino Nano

**Note**
If you have trouble uploading the code double check that you have selected the correct board and port.

### Step 8: Final Assembly

- 1x assembled CoreXY with Base Enclosure, Controls and finished wiring
- 1x Sand-Top
- Acrylic glass/Plexiglass
- cutter knife
- cutting mat
- 1x Magnetic ball - 8 mm

1. ***Carefully*** cut the **acrylic glass** so it fits onto the top inner edge of the **Sand-Top**.
2. Place your **Sand-Top** ontop of the **Corner Holders**. If it doesn't fit one way, turn it 90 degrees and try again.
3. Place the **magnetic ball** onto the sand, directly above the **Magnet Holder**.
5. Place the acrylic glass onto the top inner edge of the **Sand-Top**.

### Step 9: Have fun!

1. Draw your first temporary sand image!
2. Shake the **Sand A Sketch** carefully to erase image.
3. Repeat!
